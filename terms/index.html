Planned Free & Kadr Pro options

Kadr has not yet been released in the Mac App Store. Subscriptions and purchase controls are planned and are not yet implemented.

Free includes screenshots and basic annotations, free forever. Kadr Pro is planned as an annual auto-renewing subscription for €24.99 per year in Austria, including VAT, with a one-time 7-day free trial. Prices in other countries and currencies will be shown by the App Store.

Payment, trial & renewal

Under the planned subscription terms, payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The 7-day trial begins when you subscribe and is available once. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Renewal is charged within the final 24 hours of the period. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the paid annual subscription begins.

Manage or cancel

Manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings. After cancellation, paid access continues until the end of the paid period.

Manage subscriptions with Apple ↗

The planned subscription works on all Macs with the same Apple Account. Family Sharing is not included. Apple handles refunds; a refunded purchase ends Kadr Pro access.
